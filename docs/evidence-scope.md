# Evidence and practical application

The learning goal is to explain a mechanism accurately, judge what it establishes about behaviour, and choose a feasible action with a relevant outcome. A biological explanation and an effective intervention answer related questions. Neither automatically proves the other.

| Topic | Research supports teaching | Practical application and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brain, mind and behaviour | Neural measurement requires interpretation; reverse inference depends on the specificity of the evidence. | Separate observation, explanation and recommendation before judging a headline. A scan is not a diagnosis of a fictional character. |
| Attention | Control, working memory, switching costs and sleep-related performance changes can affect a task. | Clarify the next task, reduce optional interruptions where feasible and leave a return marker. Personal observations do not isolate a neural cause. |
| Learning | Memory uses interacting systems; training-related plasticity does not establish broad transfer. Retrieval and distributed practice have behavioural evidence. | Practise the capability with feedback and a later, unfamiliar example. Improvement on a game is not a general brain upgrade. |
| Motivation | Reward prediction errors and habits offer specific accounts of learning and action. | Examine cues, consequences, opportunity and burden. A dopamine account does not establish a person's neurotransmitter level or validate deprivation routines. |
| Emotion and recovery | Appraisal, interacting systems, stress conditions and sleep contribute to an account. | Compare clarification, practical changes and feasible recovery. A wearable signal alone does not identify emotion or establish recovery. |
| Problem-solving | Incubation and walking findings depend on tasks and comparisons; network studies describe associations and interactions. | Prepare a problem, take an optional accessible break, return and check the result. Idea quantity, correctness and usefulness are distinct outcomes. The popular “Jack Reacher” label is not a validated protocol. |

The course links primary studies, reviews and OpenStax sections in optional research disclosures. The source-check file verifies DOI metadata; this is a bibliographic check, not evidence of an exhaustive review. Open reading links were checked separately. Fictional examples and diagrams are teaching materials, not participant data. Suggested personal trials are applications to assess, not interventions established by those studies for every learner.

Independent academic review and learner testing remain outstanding. The checks in `qa/` assess content alignment, browser behaviour, accessibility, rendering and automated speech screening; they do not establish educational effectiveness.
